# Contacts

A **Contact** represents a real-world individual or external organization associated with physical infrastructure, manufacturer support channels, software licensing vendors, or internal team escalations.

## Fields

### Comments

Additional comments about this contact.

### Description

Optional notes describing the contact context.

### Email

Primary contact email address.

### Name

The contact's display name.

**Required:** Yes.

### Phone

Primary telephone number.

### Title

The contact's job title or role description.

### Web URL

Website page link.
